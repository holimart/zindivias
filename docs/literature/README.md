# Literature and prior art

The competition’s open literature review is in [`completeness-prior-art.md`](completeness-prior-art.md). It covers road and building completeness, VGI/intrinsic quality, provider/schema documentation, the organizer’s published reference-method context, and leaderboard-oracle ethics. Each entry includes a two-line applicability note and maps to one or more named competition backlog rows.

**URL checks:** 16 requested URLs checked on 2026-10-24; 15 confirmed to resolve to their cited publication host or official documentation. Some publisher sites returned access-denied/anti-bot responses, so URL resolution is distinguished from full-text verification. The Biljecki et al. DOI returned only a redirect and is explicitly marked unconfirmed and excluded from the verified count.

**Scope and use:** this is documentation-only research. No removed reference data, companion datasets, labels, or leaderboard information were obtained or used. The review informs model hypotheses and interpretation but transfers no literature-derived rates, labels, or coefficients to scored candidates. See the source document for the competition-specific limits and applicability details.
